# Meditations

Thinking that has not converged.

`docs/` describes only what is true now. `proposals/` describes a thing that has been argued
into a shape and is waiting to be built. This folder is for what comes before either: the
questions about interaction, composition and what the tool *is* that are still being turned
over.

Rules, such as they are:

1. **Nothing here is a decision.** A meditation that reaches a decision stops being one: the
   decision moves to `docs/decisions.md`, or it grows a shape and moves to `proposals/`.
2. **Write the question, not the answer.** A meditation earns its place by making a question
   sharper, or by finding the fork nobody had noticed. Confident prose that settles something
   is in the wrong folder.
3. **Ground it.** An observation about silvia or about this codebase should cite the file. The
   point of writing these down is to stop rediscovering the same thing from memory.
4. **Contradiction is fine.** Two meditations may disagree. Two decisions may not.
5. **They are allowed to die.** A meditation that turns out to be wrong gets deleted with a
   line in the commit message saying why. That is a result.
