Random noise hash for GLSL

Used in src/nodes/distort.rs: hashBits, hashBits2 and hashRandom, ported from silvia's
shaderUtils.HASH_RANDOM.

From the Stack Overflow answer by Spatial (2013) to "Random / noise functions for GLSL":
https://stackoverflow.com/questions/4200224/random-noise-functions-for-glsl

The hash itself is Bob Jenkins' one-at-a-time hash. The answer pairs it with building a float
in [0, 1) from the hash's bits.
